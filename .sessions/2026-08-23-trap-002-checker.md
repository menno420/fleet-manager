**Scope is deliberately narrow, because a noisy instrument gets ignored:**

- Scans **executable surfaces only** — `.github/workflows/*.yml|yaml`, `**/*.sh`.
- **Prose is not scanned.** fm #915 counted 26 of 389 session cards restating
