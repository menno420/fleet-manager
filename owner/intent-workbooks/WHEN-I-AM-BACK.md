## The problem this solves

`DERIVED`: your plan has a gap at its far end, not at its start. A week of
writing produces answers spread across up to 71 worksheets. The first session
back will want to be helpful and will do the most expensive possible thing —
read all of them and start "integrating" — which is how a week of your words
gets summarised into an agent's paraphrase and quietly loses the wording that
made it worth writing.

`VERIFIED`: the estate already decided the correct handling once. The session
that landed your first answered worksheet recorded it as a rule: completed
workbooks stay **verbatim owner evidence** until a later, explicit synthesis
pass promotes their settled meaning. Nothing enforces that rule yet; this page
is the enforcement.

## Paste this into your first session back

```
Read owner/intent-workbooks/PROGRESS.md first, then read only the worksheets
it marks answered. Do not read the unanswered ones.

For each answered worksheet, in one pull request:
  1. Quote my words forward verbatim. Never paraphrase, tidy, translate or
     merge them.
  2. Say what each answer CONTRADICTS in the current records — that is the
     valuable part, not the agreements.
  3. Where an answer settles something, propose the one canonical home for it
     and leave a pointer in the worksheet. Propose; do not move it yet.
  4. List every place my answer makes an existing DERIVED or PROPOSED line
     wrong, and fix those lines.
  5. Tell me which answers changed your plan and which changed nothing.

Do not start implementation work off the back of this. Report first.
```

## Restarting the subscriptions

`PROPOSED`: bring back **one** first and use it for the pass above. That pass
is documentation work with a lot of judgement and no implementation, which is
the profile you have said Claude is preferred for. Adding the others before the
answers are integrated means several agents working from records that are about
to change.
