# Questions about you, not about the estate

`DERIVED`: the existing collection asks 47 questions about repositories,
folders and the estate, and almost none about **you**. That is backwards for
the goal you stated on 2026-08-31 — *"I want to make sure that my agents
understand how I work and what I expect from them"* — because an agent that
knows every repository and nothing about how you think still guesses at the
part that matters.

`VERIFIED`: `docs/owner-profile.md` and `docs/intent.md` already carry some of
this, from the 21-question interview of 2026-08-08. Those records are good but
they are **an agent's write-up of a conversation**. These pages are for your
own hand, and where the two disagree, yours wins.

- [`how-you-work.md`](how-you-work.md) — rhythm, attention, what a good session
  with you actually looks like.
- [`how-you-want-to-be-talked-to.md`](how-you-want-to-be-talked-to.md) — length,
  language, tone, how much evidence.
- [`how-you-decide.md`](how-you-decide.md) — what you want to decide yourself
  and what you want decided for you.
- [`what-you-want-to-learn.md`](what-you-want-to-learn.md) — the split between
  skills you want and chores you want gone.
- [`what-frustrates-you.md`](what-frustrates-you.md) — the highest-value page
  in this folder. Every entry here becomes a prevented repeat.
- [`your-vocabulary.md`](your-vocabulary.md) — your words for things, so file
  and folder names can match them.
- [`time-money-and-limits.md`](time-money-and-limits.md) — hours, budget, and
  what an agent may spend without asking.
