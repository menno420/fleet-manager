# How to answer these — read this once, it takes two minutes

> **Status:** `owner-guidance` · written 2026-09-01 for the week you plan to
> spend on these with every AI subscription paused.

## The only thing you have to remember

Start any answer you write with the literal marker `OWNER:` on its own, or
inside a line as `(OWNER 01-09-2026: …)`. Everything after it is your words and
no agent may rewrite them.

That is the whole convention. It exists for one reason: **during your offline
week no agent is running, so nothing is watching these files.** When the
subscriptions come back, a session has to find your answers across ~75 files
without re-reading all of them and without guessing which paragraphs are yours.
A marker makes that a two-second search; without one it is a judgement call on
every page, and judgement calls are where this estate's mistakes live.

Your first completed page (`estate/why-this-estate-exists.md`) used
`(Owner reply 31-08-2026: …)`. That form is **also detected** — you do not have
to change how you write. Both are matched case-insensitively.

## What you do not have to do

- **No order.** Open whatever page interests you.
- **No completeness.** One answered question out of six is progress, not a
  half-done page.
- **No formatting.** Bullets, one line, three paragraphs, Dutch, English, a
  mix — all fine. Nothing is parsed except the marker.
- **No deleting.** Leave the `DERIVED` and `PROPOSED` lines in place even when
  you disagree; contradicting them in your own words is more useful than a
  clean page, because it tells the next agent *what it got wrong*, not only
  what is right.
- **No tools.** Everything here is plain Markdown, editable in any text editor
  or straight in GitHub's web editor on a phone.

## What the labels mean

| Label | Who wrote it | May an agent change it? |
|---|---|---|
| `VERIFIED` | An agent, checked against the live account or repo, dated | Only with a newer check |
| `DERIVED` | An agent's reading of your intent | Yes — that is the point |
| `PROPOSED` | An agent's recommendation, not a decision | Yes |
| `OWNER` | **You** | **Never.** Quoted forward verbatim |

## The one thing worth saying out loud

If a page's questions are the wrong questions, write that instead of answering
them. *"This is not what matters about this repo — what matters is X"* is the
single most valuable sentence you can leave in any of these files.
