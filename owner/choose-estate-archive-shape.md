## How I see it

`DERIVED` from settled decisions: a tool surfaces files untouched for 30 days;
a person judges whether their value is gone; nothing moves itself.

`DERIVED`: a same-shaped mirror looks simple but creates a second tree that will
drift. It also pollutes search and asks people to remember whether they need the
live or archived copy.

## What I suggest

`DERIVED`: freeze archived paths as
`archive/<original-role>/<YYYY-MM>/<remaining-path>`. Do not move them when the
live tree later changes.

`DERIVED`: generate one manifest recording the old path, new path, date, reason,
replacement, commit, rewritten links, and any external-reference stub. Exclude
`archive/` from normal search; search it only on purpose.

`DERIVED`: let the script prove only that a file is old and has no references
from declared active roots. Call that an **archive candidate**, never “unused”
or “value gone.”

## One guiding question

`DERIVED`: when you need something old, would you rather remember its former
folder or search one generated manifest?
